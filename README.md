### Overview

This lightweight mod allows you to take full control over your character's progression speed by dynamically scaling experience points earned in-game. Whether you want a challenging, slow-paced grind or a fast-track power play, you can easily adjust your experience rates directly through the built-in options menu.

### Features

* **Customizable Multipliers:** Fine-tune your experience gain from **25%** (Extremely Slow) up to **200%** (Double EXP) with multiple pre-set intervals.
* **Minimum EXP Failsafe:** Built-in safeguards ensure that any action granting base experience will always award at least **1 EXP**, preventing low-yield actions from rounding down to zero at reduced rates.
* **Seamless Integration:** Uses clean function wrapping to apply multipliers dynamically on experience gain events without breaking base game logic.
* **Easy Options Menu:** Change your preferred progression speed on the fly using a standard configuration menu.

### Available Rates

* **25%** – Extremely Slow
* **50%** – Half EXP *(Default)*
* **75%** – Slightly Slower
* **100%** – Vanilla Rate
* **125%** – Slightly Faster
* **150%** – Fast
* **175%** – Very Fast
* **200%** – Double EXP
